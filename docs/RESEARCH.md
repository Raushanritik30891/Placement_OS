# Indian Fresher Placement Research

**Status: research framework only.** A completed evidence-backed 2026–2027 company matrix has not yet been assembled.

## Employers in scope
Mass hiring/service: TCS (Ninja, Digital, Prime/NQT), Infosys, Wipro, HCLTech, Accenture, Cognizant, Capgemini, LTIMindtree, Tech Mahindra, Deloitte, IBM, DXC Technology and Persistent Systems.

Product/technology: Amazon, Microsoft, Google, Adobe, Oracle, Walmart Global Tech, Flipkart, Salesforce, Cisco and Qualcomm.

## Evidence hierarchy
1. Official employer career pages and job descriptions.
2. Official assessment or candidate documentation.
3. Employer recruitment announcements.
4. Credible technical assessment references.
5. Public candidate reports, clearly labelled anecdotal.

## Required record per hiring claim
Employer and role; graduation year and eligibility; assessment sections and format; verified coding count/language/difficulty if documented; interview stages; official application/assessment URL; source title and URL; date checked; confidence level; evidence type (official, candidate-reported, common practice or original platform exercise).

## Rules
- Do not invent question counts, cutoffs or assessment formats.
- Do not call a practice problem an actual company question unless the source supports that attribution.
- Candidate experiences are anecdotal, not official policy.
- Recheck eligibility and links for each hiring cycle.

## Limitation
Company-by-company verification is pending. Treat employer-specific content as pending until source records are collected and reviewed.
